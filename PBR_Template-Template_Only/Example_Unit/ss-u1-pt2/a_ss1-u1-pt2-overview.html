Unit 1: Time and Place: Landmarks & Communities - Part 2

Weeks 4-7

1st Guiding Question

What is the significance of places within communities?

1st Learning Outcome

Students explore some of the major physical features of our world.

1st Skills and Procedures

  • Think of ways to tell the difference between natural and made-by-people landmarks.
  • Talk about why a special place in your community is important to you.
  • Draw maps of Alberta and show where the natural landmarks are. If you can, also write their names in Indigenous languages.
  • Learn the names of landmarks in Alberta, including the natural ones, in the languages of the First Nations, Metis, and Inuit people.

2nd Guiding Question

How can sharing cultures and histories build connections between communities?

2nd Learning Outcome

Students explore cultures and histories build connections between communities.

2nd Skills and Procedures

  • Identify expressions of culture in the local community
  • Describe expressions of culture in a Francophone community
  • Select and share an expression of personal culture
  • Compile information about different cultures in the local community
  • Identify diverse cultures of First Nations, Metis, and Inuit communities
  • Illustrate how First Nations, Metis, and Inuit communities connect to the land
  • Discuss First Nations, Metis, and Inuit support of conservation
