What is culture?

Culture is how people live and share their special ways, stories, and celebrations.

Language is how we communicate. It can be spoken or written and varies from place to place. Different communities may speak different languages or dialects.

Traditions are special activities or customs that people do regularly. These can include family gatherings, special meals, or rituals.

Beliefs are what we think is true about the world and life. They can influence how people act and what they value. As Christians, we believe in the existence of one God, Jesus Christ as the Son of God and saviour, and the Holy Spirit, as well as the belief in the Bible as God's inspired word.

Knowledge includes the skills and information passed down through generations. This may include storytelling, cooking, or crafting.

Celebrations are special events that often mark important occasions like birthdays, holidays, and community festivals.

Arts include music, dance, painting, and other fun ways to be creative. They show the feelings and traditions of people.
