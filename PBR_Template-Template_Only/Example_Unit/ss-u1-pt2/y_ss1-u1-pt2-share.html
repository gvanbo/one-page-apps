Proverbs 22:1 "A good name is to be chosen rather than great riches, and favour is better than silver or gold."

Some common Biblical names have significant meanings:

  • Aaron - "high mountain" or "exalted"
  • Benjamin - "son of the south"
  • Candace - "queen mother"
  • Daniel - "beloved"
  • Esther - "star"
  • Hannah - "favour, grace"
  • Jesse - "gift"
  • Levi - "joined, attached"
  • Moses - "son"
  • Rebekah - "join, tie"
  • Sarah - "princess, lady"
  • Timothy - "honouring God"
A name demonstrates authority, intimacy, and character and can even communicate God's message. Do you know what your name means? Ask your parents!
