Week 8: Days 1-3 Lesson 13: Activity: Cultural Show and Tell

Read or Listen


Have your supervisor read pages 71 - 72 in this Student Instructional Binder to prepare for a presentation.

Do


Using pages 73-74 of the Student Workbook Binder, plan a presentation to give by video to submit for this assignment.

Use page 76 as a rubric to complete as a supervisor and student self-reflection activity to include with the video presentation and explanation.

You may wish to use page 10 (1TP2.3 My Culture) to record some of your own cultural experiences and ideas.

Discuss

  • What is culture, and why is it important to learn about different cultures?
  • How do traditions and customs help us understand the people in our community?
  • In what ways can we celebrate and respect the different cultures around us?
  • How might learning about other cultures change the way we see our own culture?
  • What is your favorite aspect of your own culture, and how can you share that with others?
