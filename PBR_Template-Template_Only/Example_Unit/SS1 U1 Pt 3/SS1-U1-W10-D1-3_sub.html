Week 10: Days 1-3 Lesson 16: The Importance of the Land & Unit Review

Read or Listen


The land is very important for food, water, and shelter. It is a place for cultural activities and ceremonies.

  • Gathering food: The First Nations, Metis, and Inuit would gather berries, plants, and nuts for food or medicine. This practice helps people know when and where to gather.
  • Harvesting food: Harvesting refers to collecting fish, meat, and crops. It is done respectfully, taking only what is needed.
  • Drying food: Drying food is a way to preserve food for the future.
  • Conservation: Conservation means protecting resources for the future.
