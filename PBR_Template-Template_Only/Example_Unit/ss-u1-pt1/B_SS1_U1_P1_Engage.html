For Social Studies this year, we will explore Alberta landmarks, relationships between people, present and historical communities, systems of organizations, and citizenship.

Do you know where you live? With your supervisor, look for your location on the Alberta map here, a globe at home, or other maps you may have.

In the Bible, in Genesis 1:1 it says, "In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth." Take a few minutes to read Genesis Chapter 1 in your Bible or the creation story in your children's Bible, or listen to your supervisor read it aloud to you. Discuss with your supervisor on which days God created the beautiful things in our world. What are some things that God created in nature that we see in Alberta?
