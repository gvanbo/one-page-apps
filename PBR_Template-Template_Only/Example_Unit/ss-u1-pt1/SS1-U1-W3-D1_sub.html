Week 3 Day 1: Lesson 3 (continued)

The Rocky Mountains are amazing evidence of God's awesome creation with breathtaking heights, the astounding symphony of falls over rocks, brilliant forests and plant life, tranquil lakes and streams, unique wildlife, and more that all testify to God's creation of the world! During the flood, tectonic plates broke and moved about smashing into each other before slowly settling into their present locations. The catastrophic forces during the Flood resulted in unique features for every mountain and mountain range. Today, the only forces shaping the Rocky Mountains landscape are the actions of rain, wind, chemical weathering, and streams. Thankfully, God keeps His promise to never flood the earth again with such a catastrophic action (Genesis 9:11).

Have you visited Jasper National Park or Banff National Park? These parks showcase the Rocky Mountains, in addition to Waterton, Yoho, Kootenay, and more! The evidence is all around us! Just as David wrote in Psalm 8, it inspires us to think about his proclamation, "O Lord, our Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth!" (v. 1). Read the entire Psalm 8 and thank God for His amazing creation He delights to share with us!
