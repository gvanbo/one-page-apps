Read or Listen


Listen to an adult read page 24 in the Student Instructional Binder.

Do


Complete page 25, dictating any needed words with your supervisor and drawing animals.

Discuss any prairie lands you are familiar with, as well as other crops and animals that make up that area. Do you live on the prairies or near the mountains? Are you familiar with any places that have historical significance to the First Nations, pioneers, or other history? Identify some Alberta mountains on your map.

Optional:
Cut out the Exit Cards (after page 25) in your Student Workbook Binder. Fill out one of the Exit Cards, and check your answers with your supervisor. Give the remaining three Exit Cards to family members or friends to test their knowledge of Alberta prairies. You can be their teacher today!
