Lesson 5: The Bow River & Lake Louise

Riddle

"I flow and sparkle, but I'm not a star, I twist and turn, and I travel far. What am I?"

I am a river!

Remember, a landmark is an important place or feature that is easy to recognize. Both mountains and rivers, and lakes are natural landmarks. In Alberta, we have many beautiful natural landmarks. You can find them on a map. A map shows us where things are, using symbols or pictures.
