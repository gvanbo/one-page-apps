Week 2 Days 1-3

Lesson 2: Model Creation OR Collage (Natural and Built Landmarks)

Read or Listen


The supervisor should read through pages 12-19 in the Student Instructional & Workbook Binders, understanding that it is written with a class in mind. Feel free to have siblings join the fun, working together in one group, if appropriate. Engage the child/children in conversation discussing natural and built landmarks using clues from the criteria on page 13 and becoming familiar with the descriptions of different landmarks on page 14.

Do

  1. Engage in discussion on planning and then the construction of your model. Use pages 15 & 16 in the Student Instructional Binder for planning discussion ideas. Do not write in the pages of this Student Instructional Binder.
  2. Complete pages 17 & 18 in your Student Workbook Binder, adding names of some of the built landmarks you designed. You may dictate responses for your supervisor to help complete this.
  3. The supervisor should complete page 19 to share explanations about the activity. Please include photos and /or a video to showcase your project.
    OR you may choose to create a Landmark Collage.
    Have your student create a collage of Alberta landmarks using pictures from magazines or printed images. Encourage them to include natural landmarks and landmarks with First Nations, Métis, and Inuit names. After completing their collage, have your child present their artwork in a video, sharing the names and significance of the landmarks they included.
