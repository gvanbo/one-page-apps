Grade 1 Social Studies

Welcome to Grade 1 Social Studies!

For Social Studies this year, we will be exploring relationships between people, present and historical communities, Alberta landmarks, systems of organizations, and citizenship. There are three units of study called Time and Place, Systems, and Citizenship. Plan to complete work about 2-3 sessions each week. Some lessons are shorter and allow some flexibility for you to work ahead.

You will be using the Student Instructional Binder for all your lessons. Please do not write in this binder, as it will be returned to the office at the end of the year. Your assignments are found in the Student Workbook Binder, which is a consumable resource. They do not need to be completed by memory, as you may want to refer to the reading/lesson in the Student Instructional Binder as you work through your assignments.

You may work at your own pace and work ahead in the program if you would like.

For each lesson, you will have a section to read and then an assignment, fun activity, or special project to complete. Your supervisor will read the lesson aloud to you and any written responses may be dictated by the student for the supervisor to write down.

You must complete all of the assignments and special projects, but only some of them will be submitted in Moodle. Instructions will be given in the lessons.

Please email your teacher if you have any questions.
