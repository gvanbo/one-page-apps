Week 9: Day 1 Lesson 25: Compare Two Regions

Discuss


  1. Share with your supervisor which region of Canada is your favourite and what you like about it.
  2. Ask your supervisor to share about their favourite Canadian region and what they like about it.
  3. Compare your two favourite regions and share how they are similar or different.
  4. Take a moment to pray and praise the Lord for how he created Canada to be special. Pray for the people who live in these two regions.
