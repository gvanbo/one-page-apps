Week 9: Day 2 Lesson 26: Sorting Game and Comparing Regions Check In

Play


  1. Cut out the region cards in your Student Workbook Binder.
  2. Place the cards face up on a table and mix them up.
  3. Together with your supervisor, take turns picking a card and matching it to the appropriate region or region name.
  4. Once you have matched them all, mix them up again and try matching all six regions on your own.

Do


  • Review the Vocabulary Words.
  • Complete the Check-In page in the Student Instructional Binder.
NOTE: This is an OPEN book Check-In. You may refer to the Vocabulary Words, map of Canada in your Student Instructional Binder, or assignments in the Student Workbook Binder as you work on this assignment.

Optional: Exit Activity:

  1. Cut out the Exit Cards in your Student Workbook Binder.
  2. Fill out one of the Exit Cards, and check your answers with your supervisor.
  3. Give the remaining Exit Cards to family members or friends to test their knowledge. You can be their teacher today and kindly grade their work.
