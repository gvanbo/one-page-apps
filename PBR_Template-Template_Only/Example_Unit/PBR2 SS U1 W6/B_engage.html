What a beautiful world God has made! He made mountains, water, birds, fish, and land animals, and He cares and provides for all of the needs of the animals. He also cares for you and provides the things that you need as well.

In the Bible, in Matthew 6: 26 it says, “Look at the birds of the air; they do not sow or reap or store away in barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?”

Here is a video called, God Cares for Us.

Be sure to ask an adult to supervise you as it is on YouTube.
