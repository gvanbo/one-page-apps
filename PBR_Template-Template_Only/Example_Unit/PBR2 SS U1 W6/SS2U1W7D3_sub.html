Week 7: Day 3 Lesson 21: Natural Resources Check In

Watch

Enjoy this video read aloud of the book My First Little House Books: County Fair.

As you watch the video, think about all of the natural resources in the story. You may want to make a list or say them out loud to your supervisor as you watch. Can your supervisor list any that you may have missed?

Be sure to ask an adult to supervise you as it is on YouTube.

3 Ways to Watch:

  1. Scan or Click the QR code.
  2. Enter this URL into your browser.
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AZEtQZmwJ0Q
  3. Search YouTube for "Fall Read Aloud Book for Kids | County Fair, My First Little House Books" by " The Story Harbor ".

Discuss


  1. Together with your supervisor, list all of the natural resources from the video. Think of as many as you can, including food, animals, wood, and more.
  2. Share your favourite part of the story with your supervisor.
  3. Which natural resources do you use today?
  4. Take a moment to pray and praise the Lord for all of the natural resources He has given you and blessed your family with. He is so good!
