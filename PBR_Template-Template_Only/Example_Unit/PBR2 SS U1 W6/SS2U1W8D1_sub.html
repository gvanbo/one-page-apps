  3. Search YouTube for "A Walk in the Arctic for Kids | Educational Video for Early Learners" by "Scholastic".

Discuss


Take a few minutes to discuss the video with your supervisor.

  1. What did you notice about the colour of many of the animals in the video?
  2. Why did God make the animals with the ability to camouflage themselves?
  3. What are some other ways that God created the animals to be able to live in the Arctic? Think of how He made their bodies with fur, etc.
  4. Take a moment to pray and praise the Lord for the Arctic animals and how he made them so special.
